Safety Planning

If you're having thoughts of suicide:
  • Call 988 - Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
  • Text HOME to 741741 - Crisis Text Line
  • Call 911 - For immediate emergencies
  • Go to the nearest emergency room
  • Stay with someone you trust
Create a safety plan:
  • Identify warning signs that you're in crisis
  • List coping strategies that help you feel better
  • Identify trusted people you can contact
  • Remove means of self-harm from your environment
  • List professional resources you can contact

You Matter
